How a decision is made

1 · LoadTyped rows from every dataset CSV; malformed rows are skipped.
2 · Read evidenceRules first; unknown messages to Gemini; trust gate and local NLI check; images by SHA-256 cache.
3 · ResolveFX at settlement date; pending debits reserved; pending credits ignored; recurring expenses and salary detected.
4 · 90-day ledgerDaily balance; safe amount today and earliest safe full-payment date.
5 · CandidatesFull, supplied installments, partial, wait; up to three spending changes.
6 · RankSafety filter, then the spec's six-step order.
7 · Explain & checkGrounded explanation; schema and consistency checks.
